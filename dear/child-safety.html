Dear (“the App”, “we”, “us”) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This document describes our standards, prevention measures and reporting procedures, in compliance with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy for Social and Dating apps and with the App Store Review Guidelines for apps with user-generated content. It applies to both the iOS and the Android version of the App.

1. Zero-Tolerance Commitment

We strictly prohibit the creation, upload, sharing, solicitation or distribution of any content that sexually exploits or abuses children within the App. This includes, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form (text, image, audio, video, AI-generated or illustrated)
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to sexualize minors
  • Trafficking, solicitation or recruitment of minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing personal contact information of minors for sexual purposes
  • Any other content or conduct that endangers the safety or well-being of a child

2. Age Restriction

The App is intended exclusively for users aged 16 and older who are in, or interested in, a romantic relationship. The App is not directed at, designed for, or marketed to children. Users under 16 are not permitted to create an account.

Store age ratings describe how suitable the App’s content is and are derived from each store’s own questionnaire; they do not lower the minimum age stated here. If we discover that an account belongs to a user below the minimum age, we will terminate the account and delete the associated data.

3. Prevention Measures

We implement the following measures to prevent CSAE/CSAM:

  • Account-gated access — all features require an authenticated sign-in (Google Sign-IniOSor Sign in with Apple). There is no anonymous or guest mode.
  • Couple-pairing model — private content (Q&A, exchange diary entries, calendar Androidand Heart Mail / chat messages) is visible only to the two paired partners. It cannot be broadcast or shared publicly inside the App.
  • No public social graph — the App does not allow discovery of, messaging with, or friend requests between strangers. A user can interact only with the single partner they invited or were invited by.
  • Content moderation — user-generated text is subject to automated and manual review when reported. Automatically generated responses are filtered to avoid sexual content involving minors.
  • Account termination — any user found violating these standards is permanently banned and, where legally required, reported to law enforcement.

4. In-App Reporting and Blocking

Users can report child-safety concerns, CSAE, CSAM or any inappropriate content directly from within the App:

  • Open the App → Settings → Report / Contact us
  • This opens your email app with a pre-filled message to our safety team
  • For child-safety issues, please begin the message with [Child Safety] so we can prioritize it
  • Describe the issue and, optionally, attach evidence

You can also email us directly at the address below. We review every report and respond within 72 hours.

Report email: sh7903@gmail.com
Subject line: [Child Safety Report] <short description>

Blocking and disconnecting

A user can end the link with their partner at any time from Settings ▸ Unlink Couple. Because the App has no public social graph and a user can be linked to only one partner, unlinking removes the single channel through which another person can reach them: once unlinked, neither party receives or can see anything the other writes from that point on, and neither can contact the other inside the App. Content shared before unlinking may remain in the other party’s copy until that person deletes it.

A user can also delete their account and all associated data outright, from Settings ▸ Delete Account.

5. Our Response Process

When a report is received, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt and begin review within 72 hours
  • Preserve the reported content and associated account data for investigation
  • Remove violating content immediately upon confirmation
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the offending account
  • Report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org, and to local authorities as required by applicable law
  • Cooperate fully with law enforcement requests for information

6. Legal Compliance

We comply with all applicable child-safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • 18 U.S.C. § 2258A (U.S. reporting requirements to NCMEC)
  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Act on the Protection of Children and Youth Against Sex Offenses (Republic of Korea)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Article 8 (child consent)
  • Google Play Developer Program Policies — Child Safety Standards
  • App Store Review Guidelines — user-generated content and safety requirements

We report suspected CSAM to NCMEC (for users in the United States and globally where permitted) and to the Korea Communications Standards Commission (방송통신심의위원회) or the Korean National Police Agency (사이버범죄 신고시스템 ECRM, ecrm.police.go.kr) for incidents involving users in the Republic of Korea.
